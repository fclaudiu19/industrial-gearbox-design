# Industrial Helical Gearbox — Mechanical Design & Analysis (CATIA V5)

A complete mechanical engineering design and stress calculation project for an industrial single-stage helical/spur gear speed reducer, developed in **CATIA V5**.

---

##  Project Overview
This repository contains the complete 3D parametric CAD assembly, kinematic analysis, subassemblies, standard component integrations, manufacturing execution drawings, and technical calculation reports.

- **CAD Platform:** Dassault Systèmes CATIA V5 (Part Design, Assembly Design, Generative Drafting, DMU Kinematics)
- **Standardized Hardware:** ISO 4017 Hex Screws, ISO 4034 Nuts, DIN standard parallel keys, deep groove ball bearings, radial shaft oil seals
- **Lubrication & Maintenance:** Oil inspection glass, oil drain plug, air breather plug, casing inspection cover

---

##  Key Mechanical Assemblies
- **Transmission:** Cylindrical pinion and gear stage
- **Input & Output Shafts:** High-torque stepped shafts with keyways
- **Housing & Structural Casing:** Split casing design with centering pins and lifting eye bolts
- **Kinematic Modeling:** Mechanism simulation model
